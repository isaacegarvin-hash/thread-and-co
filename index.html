Our story

Fewer pieces, better made.

Worn hard, mended often, kept for years.

Thread & Co. started as a small studio making clothes we actually wanted to wear: honest fabrics, unfussy cuts, and details that hold up after a hundred washes.

There is no seasonal drop and no filler. Each piece is cut to sit easy, in cloth we would rather mend than replace.

  • Natural fibers

    Cotton, merino, and linen chosen for breath and drape.

  • Considered fits

    Cut to sit easy, not oversized for the sake of it.

  • Repair, don’t replace

    Built to be mended, not discarded after a season.
